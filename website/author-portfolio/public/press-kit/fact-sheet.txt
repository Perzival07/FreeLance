BOOKS
------------------------------------------------------------

    When the last lamp of a drowned city flickers, one keeper must decide what the dark is owed.
  A City of Paper Moths (2021) - Lanternfall Trilogy #2, 432 pp.
    A city made of pages begins to burn, and the only witness is a girl who cannot read.
  The Last Wick (2023) - Lanternfall Trilogy #3, 456 pp.
    The final flame is nearly spent, and the whole drowned world must agree what light is worth.
  Salt & Saint Elsewhere (2022) - standalone, 312 pp.
    One morning the sea simply leaves, and a village learns what it was really holding on to.
  The Cartographer’s Daughter (forthcoming 2027-03-14) - standalone, 408 pp.
    A mapmaker’s child inherits a map of places that do not exist yet, and one of them is on fire.

SERIES
------------------------------------------------------------

light. Three novels of grief, wonder, and stubborn hope, each telling the same
story from a different depth.
