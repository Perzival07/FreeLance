- Horror author sites .. high contrast, shadows, micro-animation, optional
                         ambient sound

Must-have sections (nearly universal):
1. Books first — hero = current/upcoming book with buy/pre-order CTA
2. Book detail pages — cover, logline, excerpt, reviews, retailer links
3. About — short bio, achievements, personality, strong headshot
4. Social proof — press/review quotes, awards
5. Newsletter with a lead magnet (free short story)
6. Events / tour dates
7. Press kit — downloadable headshots, short/long bio
8. Contact + social links
9. Mobile-first, accessible, fast

Creative techniques to borrow:

- Cover-first landing that unfolds

PART C — DESIGN SYSTEM
================================================================================

Mood: "an old library at night, lit by a single lantern" — warm, inky, literary,
with moments of gold light. Day mode = parchment & ink. Night mode = ink & candle.

Colour tokens (CSS custom properties on :root, overridden in [data-theme="night"]):
  DAY                          NIGHT
  --bg        #F4ECDD parchment  #0F0D0B ink-black
  --bg-alt    #EADFCB            #1A1612
  --text      #1E1A16 ink        #EFE6D6
  --text-soft #5A5048            #A99D8C
  --accent    #B4472B vermilion  #E8A84A lantern-gold
  --accent-2  #2F4A3E bottle-green #6FA58C
  --line      rgba(30,26,22,.15) rgba(239,230,214,.12)
  Respect prefers-color-scheme on first visit; remember choice in localStorage
  (wrapped in try/catch).

Typography (Google Fonts):
  Display ...... "Fraunces" (variable, use SOFT/WONK axes for character)
  Body ......... "Newsreader" or "EB Garamond"
  UI / labels .. "Inter Tight" (small caps, letter-spaced)
  Fluid type scale with clamp(); hero name ~ clamp(3.5rem, 12vw, 11rem)

Motion principles:
  - Easing: "expo.out" / "power3.out"; durations 0.6–1.2s
  - Everything reveals on scroll (ScrollTrigger), staggered
  - prefers-reduced-motion: disable Lenis, parallax, cursor trail, page-turns;
    content must be fully visible without JS animations
  - Never block content behind animation longer than ~1.5s (preloader cap)

Texture: subtle SVG paper-grain noise overlay (fixed, pointer-events:none, ~4% opacity)

Layout: 12-col grid, max-width 1440px, side gutter 16px mobile / 48px desktop.
Breakpoints: 480, 768, 1024, 1440.
