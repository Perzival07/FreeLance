Frequently Asked Questions

Message us on WhatsApp with your order and preferred pickup time, ideally the evening before or first thing in the morning — we'll confirm availability and have it boxed and ready at the counter.

Yes — we offer vegan breads, a gluten-free seed loaf, and clearly tag nuts on our menu. Our kitchen handles gluten, nuts, and dairy, so please flag any allergies when ordering.

Walcot Street is pedestrian-friendly with limited on-street parking. The nearest public car park is Podium, a five-minute walk away, and the bakery is well served by local bus routes.

We do — custom cakes start from £28 and need at least 72 hours' notice. Message us on WhatsApp with your date, size, and flavour preferences to get a quote.

Our sourdough and croissants regularly sell out by mid-morning on weekends. For guaranteed stock, reserve ahead on WhatsApp or arrive shortly after we open at 06:30.
