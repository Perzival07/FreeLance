Our Story & Philosophy

The Art of Slow Fermentation

Dedicated to heritage craftsmanship, wild starters, and time-honored baking methods.

We are an independent neighborhood bakery on Walcot Street in Bath. Every morning at dawn, we bake wild-fermented sourdough loaves, crisp viennoiserie, and seasonal buns for our local community.

Our bake begins with just four raw ingredients: stoneground flour from Somerset mills, filtered water, sea salt, and our living wild starter. We give each dough a 36-hour slow cold ferment for deep blistered crusts, open custardy crumb, and natural digestibility.
