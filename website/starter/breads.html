Bath, England

Country Sourdough

Our founding loaf — stoneground white and wholemeal flour, wild-leavened over 36 hours for a deep blistered crust and open, custardy crumb.

36-hr ferment 800g Vegan
Bake it at home

Country Sourdough

400g strong white flour · 100g wholemeal · 360g water · 100g active starter · 10g salt

  1. Mix the flours and water, rest 1 hour, then work in the starter and salt.
  2. Stretch and fold four times over 2 hours; leave until risen by about half.
  3. Shape, proof overnight in the fridge, and bake in a lidded pot at 240°C: 20 min covered, 25 uncovered.
Made for
  • Avocado toast with chilli
  • Panzanella
  • Ribollita
Somerset Mill

Somerset Rye Loaf

A dense, dark rye blend with toasted caraway, milled three miles from our hatch. Slow-proofed for a tight crumb and malty depth.

24-hr ferment 700g Vegan Wholegrain
Bake it at home

Somerset Rye Loaf

300g dark rye flour · 300g water · 150g rye starter · 8g salt · 1 tsp caraway

  1. Stir everything into a thick paste. Rye needs no kneading.
  2. Pack into a greased loaf tin and smooth the top with a wet hand.
  3. Proof 2–4 hours until the surface cracks, bake at 220°C for 50 min, and rest a day before slicing.
Made for
  • Smoked salmon & dill
  • Reuben sandwich
  • Cheddar & pickle
West Country Grain

Seeded Wholemeal

100% stoneground wholemeal, crusted in sunflower, linseed, and pumpkin seed. Nutty, dense, and built to toast beautifully on day two.

30-hr ferment 750g Vegan Contains Seeds
Bake it at home

Seeded Wholemeal

450g wholemeal flour · 50g mixed seeds, plus extra for the crust · 380g water · 100g starter · 9g salt

  1. Soak the seeds in 50g of the water for an hour.
  2. Mix everything, knead lightly, and leave to rise for 4–5 hours.
  3. Shape, roll in seeds, proof in a tin overnight in the fridge, and bake at 220°C for 40 min.
Made for
  • Poached eggs & spinach
  • Hummus & roast veg
  • Peanut butter & banana
Bath, England

Focaccia of the Day

Olive-oil-rich dough, dimpled and topped daily — rosemary sea salt, or roast tomato and olive when the counter allows.

18-hr ferment Whole tray Vegan
Bake it at home

Focaccia of the Day

500g strong white flour · 400g water · 100g starter · 10g salt · 60ml olive oil

  1. Mix to a wet dough and stretch and fold three times over 90 minutes.
  2. Rest overnight in the fridge in a well-oiled tray.
  3. Dimple with oily fingers, top with rosemary and flaky salt, and bake at 230°C for 25 min.
Made for
  • Mortadella & burrata sandwich
  • Torn beside antipasti
  • Dipped in tomato soup
French Tradition, Bath-Baked

Sourdough Baguette

A naturally leavened take on the French classic — thin crisp shell, irregular open crumb, no commercial yeast in sight.

20-hr ferment 250g Vegan
Bake it at home

Sourdough Baguette

500g strong white flour · 350g water · 100g starter · 10g salt

  1. Mix, fold three times, then cold-ferment overnight.
  2. Divide into three, rest 20 min, and roll each into a long baton.
  3. Proof 1 hour, score diagonally, and bake at 250°C with steam for 22 min.
Made for
  • Jambon-beurre
  • Garlic bread
  • French onion soup croûtes

Gluten-Free Seed Loaf

A buckwheat and teff blend developed with a coeliac regular in mind — dense, nutty, and baked in a dedicated tin to avoid cross-contact.

12-hr ferment 600g Gluten-Free Vegan
Bake it at home

Gluten-Free Seed Loaf

200g buckwheat flour · 100g teff flour · 40g psyllium husk · 60g mixed seeds · 450g warm water · 7g dried yeast · 8g salt

  1. Whisk the psyllium into the water until it gels.
  2. Stir in everything else to a thick dough and press into a lined tin.
  3. Rise 1 hour, bake at 210°C for 60 min, and cool completely before slicing.
Made for
  • Smashed avocado
  • Toasted with nut butter
  • Cheese board
Enriched, Butter-Laminated

Sourdough Brioche Loaf

A naturally leavened brioche, enriched with cultured butter and egg — pillowy, faintly sweet, exceptional as French toast.

28-hr ferment 550g Contains Egg & Dairy
Bake it at home

Sourdough Brioche Loaf

400g strong white flour · 4 eggs · 150g starter · 50g sugar · 8g salt · 200g soft butter

  1. Knead the flour, eggs, starter, sugar and salt for about 10 min until smooth.
  2. Work in the butter a little at a time until glossy, then chill overnight.
  3. Shape into a tin, proof until doubled (4–6 hours), egg-wash, and bake at 180°C for 35 min.
Made for
  • Brioche French toast
  • Bread & butter pudding
  • Prawn roll

Ancient Grain Miche

A large-format miche made with heritage einkorn and spelt, milled fresh weekly. Earthy, complex, and keeps well beyond a week.

40-hr ferment 1.2kg Vegan Weekends Only
Bake it at home

Ancient Grain Miche

500g spelt flour · 300g einkorn flour · 620g water · 160g starter · 18g salt

  1. Mix gently. Ancient grains have weaker gluten, so go easy.
  2. Rise for 5 hours with two folds, then shape one large round.
  3. Proof overnight in the fridge and bake at 230°C for 60 min, lid on for the first 25.
Made for

Ciabatta Rolls

Bake it at home

Sourdough Pretzel Rolls

500g strong white flour · 280g water · 100g starter · 30g butter · 10g sugar · 10g salt · baking soda for the bath

  1. Knead a stiff dough, rise for 3 hours, and shape tight rolls.
  2. Proof 1 hour, chill 20 min, then dip each for 30 sec in simmering water with 1 tbsp baking soda per litre.

Bake it at home

English Muffins

450g plain flour · 300g milk · 100g starter · 20g butter · 10g sugar · 8g salt · semolina for dusting

Japanese Milk Bread

Impossibly soft and pillowy, made using the Japanese tangzhong technique for an ultra-tender crumb.

16-hr ferment 600g Contains Dairy & Egg
Bake it at home

Japanese Milk Bread

Tangzhong: 25g flour + 125g milk · Dough: 350g bread flour · 120g milk · 1 egg · 40g sugar · 7g dried yeast · 6g salt · 40g butter

  1. Cook the tangzhong to a thick paste and let it cool.
  2. Knead everything except the butter, work the butter in until silky, and rise for 1 hour.
  3. Roll up three pieces side by side in a tin, proof until doubled, and bake at 175°C for 30 min.
Made for

  • Thick-cut buttered toast

Baker's Notes

Getting the Most from Your Loaf

The questions we're asked most at the counter, answered properly.

No. 01

Storing It

Keep the loaf cut-side down on a wooden board, or in a linen or paper bag. Never the fridge, which stales bread faster than the counter does.

No. 02

Reviving Day Three

Run the crust briefly under the tap, then give it eight to ten minutes in a hot oven at 200°C. The crust crackles again and the crumb softens.

No. 03

Freezing Well

Slice it first, freeze it on the day you buy it, and toast straight from frozen. A long-fermented loaf freezes better than most.

No. 04

Why the Long Ferment

Thirty-six hours gives the wild yeast and bacteria time to work through the flour. That builds the flavour and the crust, and many people find it gentler to digest.
