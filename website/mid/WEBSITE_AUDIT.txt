LOKNATH SOFA CENTER — WEBSITE AUDIT & REVAMP PLAN
=================================================
Date: 15 Oct 2026
Scope: customer site (frontend/). Admin panel and backend only where they affect customers.

Reference sites studied:
  WoodenStreet, IKEA India, Magnolia Home, Nitori India, Godrej Interio,
  Home Centre (Mumbai store locator), Urban Ladder (Mumbai stores),
  Durian (Goregaon store page), Pepperfry (Mumbai stores — page blocked automated
  access; reviewed via public info on Studio Pepperfry).

Note: the references are national chains. Loknath is a single showroom in
Bidhan Park, Barasat (Kolkata). Ideas below are adapted to a one-store local
business — e.g. "store locator" becomes one strong "Visit Our Showroom" page.

Priority key:  [P1] do first / high impact   [P2] next   [P3] nice to have


######################################################################
PART A — WHAT IS BAD IN THE CURRENT WEBSITE
######################################################################

A1. BUGS (things that are actually broken)
------------------------------------------
[P1] Product page main image is 16px wide on mobile.
     pages/Product.jsx: wrapper is "w-4 sm:w-[80%]" (w-4 = 1rem). On phones the
     main photo is basically invisible. Should be "w-full sm:w-[80%]".
[P1] Search icon does nothing outside /collection.
     Navbar sets showSearch=true, but SearchBar only renders on /collection.
     Clicking search on Home/Product pages appears dead. Should open search
     anywhere and take the user to results.
[P1] Filters, search and sort only work on products already loaded.
     Products load 20 at a time; Collection filters/sorts the loaded slice in the
     browser. With >20 products, "Low to High" and search give wrong/incomplete
     results. Filtering/sorting/search must be done by the API.
[P1] Newsletter form does nothing (onSubmit just preventDefault). Users enter an
     email and nothing happens — no message, nothing saved. Appears on Home,
     About and Contact.
[P2] Prices are not formatted: "₹ 45000" instead of "₹45,000".
[P2] Category/type filter checkboxes are hard-coded. If the admin adds a new
     category it never appears in filters (and old ones show even if empty).
[P2] Product grids use the array index as React key (LatestCollection,
     BestSeller) — causes wrong re-renders when lists change.
[P3] Profile dropdown is hover-only — doesn't open on touch devices/tablets in
     landscape (sm+ widths).

A2. TRUST / CONTENT PROBLEMS
-----------------------------
[P1] "Admin Panel" button in the public navbar and mobile menu. Customers should
     never see this; it looks unprofessional and invites attacks. Remove it
     (admin is reachable at /admin directly).
[P1] "We provide 24/7 customer support" (OurPolicy) contradicts the stated hours
     (Mon–Sun 10–8, Thu 11–5). False claims hurt trust.
[P1] Only 2 vague "policy" points (Quality Assurance, Customer Support). All
     references show 4–6 concrete promises: delivery, warranty, returns,
     EMI/payment, assembly, customisation.
[P1] No Returns/Refund, Shipping/Delivery, Warranty, or Terms & Conditions pages.
     Only a Privacy Policy. These are expected (and required by payment gateways
     like Razorpay/Stripe).
[P1] No customer reviews/testimonials on the homepage, no Google rating shown.
     Every reference uses social proof ("Their Words, Our Pride", star ratings).
[P2] Typo: "WHY CHOSE US" → "WHY CHOOSE US" (About page).
[P2] Footer credit "Designed by Koustav Das with ❤️" is the only line in the
     footer bottom — replace with © Loknath Sofa Center, year, GSTIN, and move
     the developer credit to small text or remove.
[P2] Exact stock count shown ("In stock (3 available)"). For furniture this is
     odd and can deter buyers; show "In stock" / "Only a few left" / "Made to
     order" instead.
[P2] Product page trust lines are generic ("100% Original product.").
[P2] Store hours inconsistent wording; Thursday hours buried in brackets.

A3. DESIGN / UX PROBLEMS
------------------------
[P1] Visual overload in the hero: Ken-Burns zoom + 3 floating colour orbs (gold,
     ruby pink, purple) + spinning text ring + shimmer text + bouncing scroll cue
     + moving marquee — all at once. Pink/purple "jewel" orbs clash with a
     furniture brand and look like a crypto site, not a sofa showroom. Every
     reference uses calm, photo-led layouts where the furniture is the hero.
[P1] Hero has no offer and no category entry points — just "Transform Your
     Home". References lead with a promo carousel + shop-by-category tiles.
[P1] Homepage has no "Shop by Category" or "Shop by Room" tiles. The only path
     to products is "Explore Collection" → one long grid.
[P1] Navigation is just HOME / COLLECTION / ABOUT / CONTACT. No product
     categories in the menu (references: Sofas, Beds, Dining, Storage... with a
     mega menu). Users can't jump to "Sofas" directly.
[P2] All-dark (near-black) theme with gold everywhere: product photos of
     light-coloured sofas/beds lose contrast; text-heavy sections are tiring.
     References are light/neutral (white, cream, warm grey) with one accent.
     Consider a warm light theme, or keep dark only for hero/footer bands.
[P2] Too many decorative animations site-wide (glow-pulse icons, shine, card
     lift, reveal on every section, two marquees). Slows phones, distracts, and
     ignores "reduce motion" preference.
[P2] Product card shows only image, name, price. Missing: MRP/discount, rating,
     "Bestseller"/"New" badge, quick add/enquire. (Nitori/Magnolia pattern.)
[P2] Product page: dimensions are printed three times (metric list, imperial
     list, summary line). One clean spec table is enough.
[P2] Mobile menu has no categories, no phone/WhatsApp, no login/account link.
[P3] Icons are PNG images (search, cart, profile) with alt text like
     "searchIcon", "pofileIcon" — use SVG icons with proper aria labels.

A4. PERFORMANCE
---------------
[P1] Hero image is 2.9 MB JPG (hero_img.jpg), contact image 1 MB PNG,
     about 280 KB. Hero alone makes mobile first load slow. Convert to WebP/AVIF,
     resize (≤1920px wide, ~200 KB), serve responsive sizes (srcset).
[P2] hero_full.jpg (545 KB) appears unused — remove dead assets.
[P2] Razorpay checkout script loads on every page (index.html). Load it only on
     the checkout page.
[P2] Product images from Cloudinary are served full size — use Cloudinary
     transformations (f_auto,q_auto,w_600) for cards.

A5. SEO & DISCOVERABILITY
-------------------------
[P1] No meta description, no Open Graph/Twitter tags (WhatsApp/Facebook shares
     show no preview), same <title> on every page.
[P1] No per-page titles ("Sofas | Loknath Sofa Center", product name on product
     pages). Use react-helmet-async or similar.
[P1] No LocalBusiness / FurnitureStore structured data (address, hours, phone,
     geo, rating) — key for "furniture shop near me" / "sofa shop Barasat".
[P2] No sitemap.xml or robots.txt.
[P2] Image alt texts are "product_img", "about_img" etc. — use the product name.
[P2] URLs use Mongo IDs (/product/66a1...). Slugs (/product/3-seater-chesterfield-sofa)
     rank and share better.
[P2] No category landing pages with text (Durian/Home Centre/WoodenStreet all
     have keyword-rich local pages: "Sofa shop in Barasat", "Furniture store
     near Madhyamgram/Kolkata").

A6. ACCESSIBILITY
-----------------
[P2] Many clickable <img>/<p> elements instead of <button>/<a> (search icon,
     menu icon, profile menu items) — not keyboard accessible.
[P2] No skip-to-content link (IKEA, Magnolia, Nitori all have one).
[P2] Filter checkboxes not wrapped in <label> — tiny click target.
[P2] Gold-on-black small text likely fails contrast in places (ivory-500 on ink).

PART C — SUGGESTED REVAMP ORDER
######################################################################
Phase 1 (fix & trust, ~quick wins)
  - Fix bugs in A1; remove Admin Panel link; remove false 24/7 claim; fix typo
  - Compress images; per-page titles + meta + OG + LocalBusiness schema
  - Floating WhatsApp, top bar with phone/hours, real footer, policy pages

Phase 2 (new look & homepage)
  - Calmer design system (decide: warm light theme vs refined dark), fewer
    animations, respect prefers-reduced-motion
  - Category navigation + new homepage sections (B2 items 1–8)
  - Showroom page (B6)

Phase 3 (shopping experience)
  - Server-side filters/search/sort, category URLs, better product cards
  - Product page upgrade (B4), pincode delivery check, enquiry buttons
  - Book-a-visit and custom-order forms + admin inbox

Phase 4 (growth)
  - Guest checkout, order tracking, WhatsApp notifications, coupons,
    blog/guides for SEO, slugs, sitemap

Information needed from the store owner before building:
  - Year established, warranty terms, return policy, delivery areas & charges
  - EMI availability, GSTIN, social media links, WhatsApp number
  - Real showroom photos, customer reviews (or Google Business link), offers
  - Whether custom/made-to-order and bulk orders are offered
